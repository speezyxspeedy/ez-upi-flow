> **Zero-KYC direct UPI intent & dynamic QR payment flow for indie builders and small businesses.**

Accept UPI payments straight into your personal or merchant VPA (`GPay`, `PhonePe`, `Paytm`, `FamPay`) without paying a **2% gateway fee** or waiting days for merchant paperwork approval.

---

### ✨ Features

- 🚀 **Zero KYC Onboarding:** No merchant portal registration. Plug in your UPI ID and start taking payments.
- 📱 **Native Mobile Intent:** Automatically summons GPay, PhonePe, and Paytm app sheets directly on mobile browsers.
- 🖥️ **Dynamic Desktop QR:** Renders high-contrast, locked-amount Base64 QR code for desktop browsers.
- ⏱️ **Strict 3-Minute TTL:** Hard countdown timer releases stuck inventory if payment isn't completed.
- 🛡️ **Anti-Back Navigation Trap:** Intercepts browser back clicks and tab closes to auto-cancel abandoned checkouts.
